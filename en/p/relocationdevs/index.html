About RelocationDevs

Decision-support platform for tech professionals choosing a city abroad: it combines expected salary, cost of living, taxes and the job market.

Key facts

  • Cost of living for 578 cities (Kaggle, Cost of Living Index 2022).
  • Salaries of 18,112 developers in 149 countries (Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2023).
  • Gross annual salary regression model: MAE of €29,022 and R² of 0.53.

Modules of the original app

  • City explorer with a relocation score and a filterable table.
  • City comparison with bar charts and a normalised radar chart.
  • Interactive map with clustering and detail popups.
  • Three-step ideal-city finder with a priority radar.
  • Salary estimator based on a Gradient Boosting model.
  • PDF report with city photos and salary distributions.

Tech stack

  • Python
  • pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • Streamlit
  • Plotly
  • Folium

Limits (stated plainly)

The model error is high because of the large geographic variation in the data (149 countries). The limitation is documented and shown to users inside the app.

What this mock-up has and what it doesn't

Yes: ranking, search with your own weights, comparison, map and salaries, using the real data and formula.

The average salary in the cost-of-living dataset is per country (the same for every city in it), not per city. Not included: the salary prediction model (Gradient Boosting), the 3-step finder with radar, or the PDF report. Those only exist in the repository.

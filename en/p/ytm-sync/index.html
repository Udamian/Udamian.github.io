Keeps a Spotify playlist (or your Liked Songs) mirrored, in the same order, on YouTube Music. A Spicetify custom app with a small local Python helper.

Playlist · What it does

  1. First-run copy of Liked Songs or a playlist to a new or existing YouTube Music playlist.
  2. On each Spotify start, new songs go to the top and changes to copied playlists are applied.
  3. Uncertain matches go to a review list with suggestions to accept, replace or skip.
  4. A “Your copies” card to switch copies off or forget them, without deleting anything on either service.

Comment
YouTube Music has no official API, so ytmusicapi may break at any time. The session is stored only on your computer and the project is not affiliated with Spotify, Google, YouTube or Spicetify.
